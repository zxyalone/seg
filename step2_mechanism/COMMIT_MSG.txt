Step 2: mechanism-level validation of PhASER-3D (+ design review)

Adds step2_mechanism/ (scripts, figures, raw outputs, two reports) and the
previously untracked validation_plan.txt.

Verification scope
- Cohort: fold-0 validation (21 cases). The 27-case test set stayed locked
  and was never read.
- CNR stratification thresholds frozen from the training split (518 lesions).

Findings
- 4.1 synthetic (3 geometries, 5 seeds): PC is exactly contrast-invariant
  (max per-voxel change 8.6e-3 for A = 5..40 HU) and |grad f| scales exactly
  with A -- but PC's advantage over |grad f| grows with CNR, not shrinks.
  C1 is falsified.
- 4.1b mechanism: PC's edge localisation comes from the Kovesi adaptive
  threshold T, not from the operator. T is an absolute-scale quantity, so at
  sigma = 5 HU, T/den reaches 1.17-1.98 and PC collapses to exactly 0 for
  CNR <= 1. Contrast invariance does not imply noise robustness.
- 4.2 real cases (87 lesions): low-CNR stratum PC boundary/peri ratio median
  0.651 (< 1), worse than |grad f| at 0.967; PC wins on only 9/36 low-CNR
  lesions. Spearman(CNR, sep_PC) = +0.53.
- 4.3 filters: learnable log-Gabor drifted 0.19-0.57 from init, but the five
  injection scales learned nearly identical banks (cross-scale lambda std
  1e-4 ~ 3e-4) in Copy2/copy1/repro0905, and lambda_3 collapsed 13.2 -> 8.5-10.7.
- 4.4 routes: route semantic selectivity (tumour vs background) is ~1e-8 and
  the relative spatial std is only 1e-3, so PFCR acts as a per-scale constant
  band-pass. Budget deviation L1 is ~0.80.
- 4.4b: pc_max_voxels=2^20 costs the shallowest skip its resolution --
  49-57% of voxels change by >1e-3 vs no cap, rank corr 0.45-0.51.

Also adds design_review.md: the 6-step argument chain with per-step verdicts
(3 hold, 2 broken, 1 unverified) and a triage of 10 implementation-level and
3 conceptual-level defects.

Known limits: synthetic noise is white Gaussian; Copy2 is epoch 48/200 so
"broken structure" cannot be separated from "undertrained"; 4.4 used 3 patches;
no ablation controls (A5 / A1 / A2) exist on this machine yet.
